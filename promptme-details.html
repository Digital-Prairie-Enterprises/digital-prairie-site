How it works

Speak. It follows.

No foot pedals. No clickers. No tapping the screen mid-sentence. Open a browser, paste your script, and start talking.

01  ·  Paste your script

Your words go in

Paste or type your script into PromptMe's editor. Short lines work best — 6 to 12 words each, one thought per line. Your scripts save automatically and sync across all your devices.

02  ·  Tap start, start speaking

PromptMe listens

Grant microphone access once. PromptMe begins tracking your voice in real time, highlighting your exact word and line as you speak. Everything runs on your device — nothing is sent to a server.

03  ·  Keep your eyes up

Stay present

When you pause, the scroll stops. When you resume, it picks up exactly where you left off. Your pace. Your pauses. Your delivery — not a timer's.

Features

Everything a speaker actually needs.

No bloat. No noise. Just the features that help you deliver better.

Speech-activated scrolling

PromptMe listens through your device's microphone and advances the script as you speak — naturally, at your pace. No hardware. No hand signals to an operator.

Word-level highlighting

Your current word is highlighted in real time. Previous lines dim. Upcoming lines glow softly ahead. You always know exactly where you are — and what's coming next.

Smart pause detection

PromptMe knows the difference between a dramatic pause and losing your place. When you stop speaking, the scroll stops. When you resume, it picks up exactly where you left off.

Works in any browser

No app to install. PromptMe runs in any modern browser — phone, tablet, laptop, or desktop. On Android and iPhone, it installs to your home screen for a full-screen, distraction-free experience.

Clean, focused design

Monospace font. Black background. Soft green or warm cream text. No clutter, no notifications, no ads. The interface disappears so you can focus on the room.

Script library

Save and organize all your scripts in one place. Sync across every device when you sign in. Your sermon series, your lecture library, your podcast catalog — always where you need it.

Who it's for

Built for real speakers with real stakes.

Not a demo tool. Not a gimmick. PromptMe is for people who speak from a script and need it to work — every time.

Pastors & Preachers

Podcasters & YouTubers
