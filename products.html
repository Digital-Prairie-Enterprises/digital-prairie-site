Clareo

Smarter billing for behavioral health.

Behavioral health billing is its own world — complex codes, documentation requirements, and the constant pressure to get it right or wait months for reimbursement. Clareo is built specifically for this world.

It reads your clinical notes, proposes the right ICD-10 and billing codes, and explains every decision in plain English — so your biller reviews and approves in moments, not minutes.

📄
Works with your existing documents
Upload notes as a PDF, Word doc, or Google Doc — or connect directly through your EHR. No new software for your providers.
⬡
AI proposes, humans approve
Every proposed code is reviewed and approved by a human before anything is submitted. Clareo handles the heavy lifting; your team stays in control.
💬
Plain-English explanations
Every code suggestion comes with a clear reason. No black-box decisions — your biller knows exactly what to look at and why.
No changes to provider documentation
Human review before every submission
