Our story

Prairie roots.
Modern tools.
Built the right way.

Digital Prairie Enterprises was born in Eastland, Texas — where folks still believe in steady work, clear thinking, and keeping your word. That's the ground we stand on, and it shapes every tool we build.

We're a small studio with a long view, building a family of apps designed to help people think straighter, map their ideas, and grow with purpose.

Where we come from

A polytechnical
homestead

Software is only the first fencepost. Digital Prairie Enterprises is laying out something bigger — a polytechnical homestead where learning, creativity, and steady growth all live under the same sky.

"Good tools should feel like good neighbors — helpful, dependable, and never in the way."

We believe technology ought to make life clearer, not louder. We believe small towns can build big things when the work is honest and the foundation is strong. And we believe the best software is the kind you forget you're using, because it just fits.

In time, this homestead will stretch beyond apps — into practical education, creative tools, training and workshops, and other work that helps folks build better lives. We're not chasing trends. We're building something meant to last.

The people
AG
Founder & Senior Architect

Allen Goodner

Eastland, Texas · 15+ years in software

Allen has deep roots in Eastland County — four generations and more have made their homes in Cisco, Eastland, and Ranger. He's a Christian, a husband, and a father, and he carries those roles with quiet pride. His family's long history in this county shows up in the way he works: steady, honest, and built for the long haul.

He's spent 15 years at the crossroads of software engineering and people leadership — guiding teams, shaping distributed architectures, and building systems that have to perform at scale. A claims engine rebuilt from 2,000 records per hour to 21,000. An integration layer that cut partner development time by 90%. Products shipped for healthcare, fintech, and retail at national scale.

Allen thinks hard about how tools should serve people, not the other way around. He's known for clear thinking, careful planning, and a knack for turning complex problems into something you can actually use.
